Featured work

Rannoch Moor, 05:42 — October 2024

The moor before
the world wakes

Three nights camped on the edge of Rannoch, waiting for the fog to sit just right. There's a particular quality of light that appears in that hour before dawn — not quite dark, not quite anything else — where the water and the heather blur into the same grey breath.


This image took a year of returning to get right. I don't believe in luck, but I believe in patience.

About the work

Each series begins with a place I feel drawn to return to — not because I know what I'll find, but because I trust something is still waiting to be seen. The camera is a reason to look slowly.

Photography is the art of observation. It has little to do with the things you see and everything to do with the way you see them.
